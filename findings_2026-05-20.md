   eliminate machine-epsilon false positives on yfinance auto-adjusted bars.

## Run 2 — Divergence Daily, US halal top-30, yfinance (first attempt)

`validation_divergence_1d_top30.json`, `logs_divergence_1d_top30.txt`.

**Verdict: 0 / 30 cleared.** Two failure modes:

1. **11/30 tickers BLOCKED on `high/low/close inconsistency`**. Investigated:
   the failure is a float64 machine-epsilon artifact (~8.88e-15 difference
   when `high == close` exactly on yfinance auto-adjusted bars). Fixed
   with scale-relative tolerance. Re-run is in progress as v2.
2. **Among the 18 that ran**: low trade counts (n ≈ 5–30) because daily
   divergences are inherently rare. **NVDA n=29 exp 1.69** and **AMGN n=29
   exp 1.98** are *one trade short* of the n≥30 gate. **ORCL n=30 exp 1.36**
   passes n but fails deflated Sharpe (-0.45 deflated, raw 0.20, 30-trial
   multiple-testing haircut).

The divergence strategy is showing positive expectancy across most tested
tickers — the failure mode is statistical certification under multiple
testing, not lack of signal.

## Cross-cutting findings

- The validation gate is doing exactly its job — rejecting low-evidence claims.
- The **binding constraint** for EMA-200 1H is **data depth**; for Divergence
  Daily it is **signal rarity + multiple-testing haircut**.
- "Edge looks present, sample insufficient" is the dominant verdict shape.
  This is the same pattern observed in the Cowork team's initial 12-ticker
  daily run, and consistent with stocks_iter Sprint R4-E memory ("friction
  eats edge" on UAE Tier-B/C — different cause, same general lesson that
  small/noisy samples don't certify).

  the most efficiently-priced equities on earth. Edge is more likely to
  survive in tier-2 large caps or in mid-caps within the halal universe.
  Plan to expand to the full 1,621-ticker list once Ahmed provides it.
